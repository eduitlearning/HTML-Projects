How Password Recovery Works

Step 1

Enter the email address registered with your PrimeNest account.


Step 2

Check your email inbox for password reset instructions.


Step 3

Follow the secure password reset process provided in the email.


Step 4

Create a new strong password and sign in to your account.


Important Password Reset Information


  • Password reset instructions will only be sent to the registered email address.

  • Check your inbox and spam or junk folder.

  • Do not share your password reset link with anyone.

  • Password reset links may expire after a limited period of time.

  • If you did not request a password reset, contact PrimeNest support.


Create A Strong Password

When creating your new password, follow these security recommendations.

  1. Use at least 8 characters.

  2. Include uppercase and lowercase letters.

  3. Include numbers.

  4. Include special characters.

  5. Avoid using your name or date of birth.

  6. Do not reuse passwords from other websites.
Protect Your Account

Keep your PrimeNest account secure by following good security practices.

  • Never share your password.

  • Do not provide your password to unknown callers.

  • Sign out from shared computers.

  • Use a trusted device when accessing your account.

  • Report suspicious activity to PrimeNest support.
