Understand Your EMI


EMI means Equated Monthly Installment.

Your monthly EMI generally depends on:

  1. Loan Amount
  2. Applicable Interest Rate
  3. Loan Tenure
  4. Loan Repayment Structure

Loan Amount Tenure Interest Structure EMI Depends On
Lower Loan Amount Shorter or Longer Varies Principal + Interest
Higher Loan Amount Depends On Eligibility Varies Principal + Interest

Actual loan eligibility, interest rates, repayment amounts and approval terms depend on lender policies and applicant eligibility at the time of application.
